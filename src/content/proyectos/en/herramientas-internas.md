## Context

Inside the agency, every project came with repeated operational work: reporting progress, uploading content, checking campaign status and moving data from one tool to another. All of it was done by hand, and each person did it their own way.

## What I did

- **Internal dashboards** that gather on one screen what used to require opening five: status of sites, campaigns and team tasks.
- **Content importers and loaders** for the high-frequency tasks: what used to be an afternoon of copy-pasting became a form and a button.
- **Integrations between the existing tools** via APIs and webhooks, so data travels on its own instead of being exported to Excel.
- **Documentation for every tool**: the goal was that anyone on the team could use them — and maintain them — without depending on me.

## Outcome

The repetitive tasks of daily operations were visibly reduced and, more importantly, standardized: the process no longer changes depending on who runs it. The tools are still in daily use inside the agency.
