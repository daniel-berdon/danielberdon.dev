## Context

Several agency clients sold online with generic templates that fell short: catalogs that changed every week loaded by hand, pricing rules the store could not express, and orders that had to be moved manually into each company’s internal processes.

## What I did

- **Custom WooCommerce plugins** for each business’s rules: special pricing, extra order fields and custom validations.
- **Catalog importers** that read the supplier’s file and update products, prices and stock with no manual work.
- **Internal dashboards** so the client can see sales, pending orders and catalog status without entering the WordPress admin.
- **Integrations with the client’s systems**: a confirmed order triggers the next steps — invoicing, logistics or a notice to the team — via webhooks.

## Outcome

The catalog stopped being loaded product by product: the weekly update went from hours to minutes. Orders flow straight into each company’s internal pipeline, and the store is no longer an isolated system that had to be fed by hand.
