## Context

A content creator with an established audience depended entirely on the platforms: if the algorithm changed, his business changed. He needed a site of his own to centralize his content, his podcast and his community.

## What I did

- **Official website** developed and administered for ten years: fast, stable and designed to turn audience into community.
- **Podcast production**: recording, editing, distribution via RSS to the audio platforms and an organized archive on the site.
- **Video production and editing** with Adobe Premiere Pro and graphic design with Photoshop for every piece on the channel.
- **YouTube integration** so new content appears on the site without duplicated work.

## Outcome

A decade of continuous, independent digital presence: the site is still the brand’s home, the podcast distributes itself and the content has a permanent archive that no platform can shut down.
